Constraints
===========

Nutrient Constraint
-------------------

This constraint is used to specify a limit on a nutrient in your food mix.

----

Nutrient Ratio Constraint
-------------------------

This constraint is used to specify a ratio between two nutrients in your food mix.

----

Food Constraint
---------------

This constraint is used to specify a limit on a food item in your food mix.

----

Food Ratio Constraint
---------------------

This constraint is used to specify a ratio between two food items in your food mix.

----

Group Constraint
----------------

This constraint is used to specify a limit on a group of food items in your food mix.

----

Group Ratio Constraint
----------------------

This constraint is used to specify a ratio between two food groups in your food mix

----

Unfeasible Solution
-------------------

When no solution exists that satisfies all the constraints specified, Snack will show "No Feasible Solution" message. This would occur if we specified two contradicting requirements, for example, that mix have 40 grams of fiber and second that mix have less than 20 grams of fiber.
