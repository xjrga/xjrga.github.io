Phytonutrients (or phytochemicals) are natural plant compounds that protect cells from damage, reduce inflammation, and lower the risk of chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, and certain cancers. Unlike essential vitamins, minerals, and macronutrients, they are not strictly required for basic survival, but eating them regularly supports long-term health.
